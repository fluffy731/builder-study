# 05 – Technical referee brief

The technical referee report is a core part of the application. Treat the referee as the
reviewer of your whole competence, not a signatory. Referee eligibility (class of registration,
years registered, independence from you) is set by the BPC form. **[Verify]**

## Choosing a referee

| Criterion | Why it matters |
|---|---|
| Holds the registration class the BPC form requires | Report may be rejected otherwise |
| Has seen your work first-hand | Specific evidence is persuasive; generic praise is not |
| Independent of you (no financial interest) | Conflict-of-interest rules **[Verify]** |
| Responsive and credible | The report is on your critical path |
| Willing to be contacted by the BPC | Assessors may follow up |

## What to give the referee

- This brief and the current BPC referee form
- A one-page career timeline
- Completed project write-ups (from `04-project-evidence-template.md`) with redacted client details
- Your target submission date and a polite deadline at least a week before it

## What a strong report addresses **[Verify against actual form]**

- How long and in what capacity the referee has known your work
- Specific projects they observed and what you personally contributed
- Competence across: contract administration, planning, compliance, site supervision, WHS,
  quality and defects, dealing with surveyors and consumers
- Any limits of the referee's knowledge (honest limits increase credibility)

## Email template (edit freely)

> Subject: Request to act as technical referee – Domestic Builder (Unlimited) application
>
> Hi [Name],
>
> I'm applying for registration as a Domestic Builder (Unlimited) with the Building and Plumbing
> Commission. As part of that I need a technical referee who has seen my work. I'd value your help
> given your experience on [project/context].
>
> The process involves completing the BPC referee form based on [N] projects I'll provide. I'd
> send the form, my project summaries and a short timeline. I'm aiming to lodge by [date], so
> ideally the report would be back by [date − 1 week]. Happy to meet or call first if that helps,
> and entirely understand if the timing or conflict rules mean you can't.
>
> Kind regards,
> [Name]

## After you receive it

- [ ] Compare the report line-by-line with your project write-ups: dates, roles, values
- [ ] Resolve discrepancies with the referee *before* lodging, not after
- [ ] Save an unaltered copy; do not edit a signed report
