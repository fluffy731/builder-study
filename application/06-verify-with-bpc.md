## Questions worth emailing or phoning the BPC
1. Which qualifications are accepted for DB-U today, including superseded codes?
2. How is "recent experience" counted – date window, full-time equivalence, supervised work?
3. How many projects, and what mix of scope is expected?
4. Can I submit before the criminal history check is issued, and how long is it valid?
5. How is domestic building insurance eligibility assessed for new registrants?
6. Is there a published study guide or reference list for the online exam?
