## What you personally did (aim for specifics)

Cover each, one or two sentences with a verifiable artefact:

1. Pre-construction: estimating, tendering, contract, permit documentation
2. Planning and programming: scheduling, procurement, subcontractor selection
3. Site management and quality: inspections, mandatory notifications, defects
4. Compliance: NCC/BCA, energy rating, structural, fire, waterproofing, drainage
5. Variations, cost control, claims, client communication
6. Safety: SWMS, induction, incidents, WHS
7. Completion: final inspection, certificates, handover, defects period
